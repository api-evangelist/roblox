Roblox released version 3 of its open-source voice safety classifier, expanding support to 22 additional languages while achieving 14% greater recall and 5% greater precision over the prior version. The model was trained on 250,000 hours of machine-labeled and 29,000 hours of human-labeled multilingual data.
